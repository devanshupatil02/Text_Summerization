📚 How It Works

A plain-English explanation of every component — useful for viva preparation.

🔍 Extractive Summarization Algorithm

Extractive summarization selects the most important sentences from the original document. No new sentences are created — it works like highlighting key lines in a textbook.

Step 1 → Split article into sentences (NLTK) Step 2 → Count word frequencies (ignore stopwords like "the", "is") Step 3 → Normalize: divide each count by max count (range 0–1) Step 4 → Score sentence = sum(word_frequencies) ÷ sentence_length Step 5 → Pick top-N sentences by score Step 6 → Return them in ORIGINAL ORDER

🤖 BART Abstractive Summarization (local version only)

BART = Bidirectional and Auto-Regressive Transformer (Facebook AI, 2020). It reads the article with its Encoder and generates new sentences with its Decoder — just like a human would. The model facebook/bart-large-cnn has 406 million parameters and requires ~4 GB RAM, so it runs locally only.

Article Text → BartTokenizer (text to token IDs) → BART Encoder (reads all tokens simultaneously, bidirectional) → BART Decoder (generates summary one token at a time) → BartTokenizer (token IDs back to text) → Abstractive Summary

📊 ROUGE Metrics

ROUGE = Recall-Oriented Understudy for Gisting Evaluation. Measures word overlap between generated summary and a human reference summary.

MetricWhat it measures
ROUGE-1Overlap of single words (unigrams)
ROUGE-2Overlap of word pairs (bigrams) — stricter
ROUGE-LLongest Common Subsequence similarity
PrecisionOf words I generated, how many were in the reference?
RecallOf words in the reference, how many did I include?
F1Balanced score = 2×P×R/(P+R) — this is what we report

📖 Viva Quick Reference

QuestionAnswer
What is BART?Bidirectional & Auto-Regressive Transformer by Facebook AI (2020). Encoder + Decoder architecture.
What is tokenization?Converting text to numbers (token IDs). "don't" = 2 tokens. BART limit = 1024 tokens.
What is attention?Mechanism that lets each word decide which other words to focus on. Core of Transformers.
What is ROUGE-1?Counts matching single words between generated and reference summary.
What is ROUGE-2?Counts matching word pairs. Stricter than ROUGE-1.
What is ROUGE-L?Longest Common Subsequence — non-consecutive word matching.
What is hallucination?Model generates fluent but factually wrong text. Known LLM limitation.
Why chunking?BART max = 1024 tokens. Long articles must be split, summarized, combined.
Extractive vs Abstractive?Extractive copies sentences. Abstractive generates new ones. Abstractive is more advanced.
Why pretrained?Training from scratch needs millions of articles + weeks on GPUs. Pretrained = free + instant.
What is CNN/DailyMail?~300k news articles with human-written summaries. Industry-standard benchmark.
Limitation of ROUGE?Measures word overlap only — not meaning, fluency, or factual accuracy.
